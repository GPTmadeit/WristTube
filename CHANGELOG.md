# Changelog

Each version's section here becomes its release notes on GitHub.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

The first release.

- Search YouTube by voice or keyboard, and play videos on the watch with the sound on its own speaker. No headphones, phone or Google account needed.
- Video at up to 240p by default (144p to 480p in Settings), sized for the round screen. Double-tap to switch between inside the circle, full width and fill.
- Sound-only mode: no video, less data and battery, and the screen can sleep while it plays. Playback carries on in the background and shows in the watch's media controls.
- The crown sets the volume. Progress runs around the edge of the screen.
- Live streams, YouTube's "up next" suggestions with optional autoplay, and more from a video's channel (long-press a result).
- Recent searches and watch history, kept on the watch.
- Fetches media in short ranged requests, which YouTube serves several times faster than one long download.
- Asks the watch for Wi-Fi or LTE while it's open or playing, and recovers by itself when stream links expire or the network changes.
- Updates itself from GitHub releases (Settings → Updates).
